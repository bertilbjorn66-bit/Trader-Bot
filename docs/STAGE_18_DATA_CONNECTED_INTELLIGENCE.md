# Stage 18 — Data-Connected Intelligence Atlas

This stage runs the intelligence controls against the preserved verified final holdout without redownloading or rewriting the source artifact.

## Outputs

- 9-pair, 4-horizon cross-pair lagged relationship summaries;
- regime-transition statistics by currency pair;
- descriptive calibration of the research agreement score against realized positive outcomes;
- transaction-cost stress at 0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 pips;
- discovery-versus-confirmation distribution drift diagnostics;
- immutable input SHA-256 binding.

## Safety

The output is descriptive intelligence only. It does not select a live strategy, does not modify the completed research gates, and cannot authorize orders.

The agreement calibration is explicitly treated as a diagnostic, not as a validated probability model. A later calibration stage must fit and validate probabilities on strictly separated data before confidence values are used operationally.
